Basic Bitrix24 advantages overview

There are several key advantages Bitrix24 offers over Raynet CRM.

1. All-in-one platform — CRM, tasks & projects, marketing, telephony, e-signature, messenger, video calls, drive and calendar in one account. The price you see is the final price: no per-user fees for core tools.

2. Flat pricing with a fixed user limit — grow the team without growing the per-user bill. Upgrade only when you exceed the user limit or need advanced features.

3. Deploy your way — cloud or On-Premise (self-hosted) for full control over data and deep customization. Raynet is cloud-only.

4. Powerful no-code automation and workflows across CRM, tasks and documents, with unlimited automation rules on Professional and Enterprise (Raynet meters automation runs).

5. Build your own apps without code — Vibecode turns a plain-language description into a working app that connects to your Bitrix24 data via unlimited REST API and the MCP Server (from Basic Vibe+).

6. Put AI to work — AI automation agents and RPA handle multi-step tasks end to end, alongside built-in AI in CRM, tasks, chats and calls (AI agents & RPA from Professional Vibe+).

Raynet CRM positioning

Raynet CRM is an easy-to-use, pipeline-focused sales CRM for sales teams that need clear opportunity tracking, strong contact and deal management and a built-in email inbox, delivered as a cloud, per-user SaaS.

Conclusion

Raynet is an easy, pipeline-focused sales CRM with growing AI (email drafting, summaries, lead scoring), but it is per-user, cloud-only and has no autonomous agents or self-hosted option.

Bitrix24 is a flat-priced all-in-one platform with AI automation agents, RPA, Vibecode no-code apps and an On-Premise edition.
