Work process

How did you start selling Bitrix24 to your clients?

We began by hosting educational webinars and offering personalized consultations. To make adoption easier, we designed small phased implementation packages, allowing companies to test Bitrix24 without taking big risks upfront.

How do you attract new clients today?

Our current strategy is built on valuable content – webinars, videos, and a private client community – combined with sector-based specialization. We work mainly with consulting firms, professional offices, and installation companies, developing tailored processes for each type of client. This makes Bit24 stand out as a reliable and distinctive partner.

Which Bitrix24 tools and resources have been the most useful?

We greatly value the direct support from the Partner Team, official training, and the materials available through the Partner Network. We also appreciate the freedom to innovate, which let us create our own Market solutions – SPA Importer and Bit24 Buttons.

Advice and Recommendations

What advice would you give to new Bitrix24 partners?

  • Don't try to be "the partner for everyone."
  • Specialize in a specific client type or industry.
  • Focus on quality over quantity.
  • Use all the resources and training available through the Partner Network.
  • Learn from your mistakes – every project is an opportunity to improve.

What mistakes did you make at the beginning, and how can they be avoided?

1

Targeting too many client profiles. That led to unfocused proposals and unsuitable projects. We learned it's better to say "no" early than to take on the wrong client.

2

Automating before understanding the client's process. We once trusted automation without validating workflows with the client first. Now, we always start with a diagnostic phase before implementing automation.
