Basic Bitrix24 advantages overview

There are several key advantages Bitrix24 offers over {{COMPETITOR}}.

1. All-in-one platform — CRM, tasks & projects, marketing, telephony, e-signature, messenger, video calls, drive and calendar in one account. The price you see is the final price: no per-user fees for core tools.

2. Flat pricing with a fixed user limit — grow the team without growing the per-user bill. Upgrade only when you exceed the user limit or need advanced features.

3. Deploy your way — cloud or On-Premise (self-hosted) for full control over data and deep customization, from interface to business logic.

4. Powerful no-code automation and workflows across CRM, tasks and documents. Professional and Enterprise tiers run unlimited automation rules.
