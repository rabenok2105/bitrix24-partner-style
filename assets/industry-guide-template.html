Quick Guide

Bitrix24 is an all-in-one platform designed to solve the key challenges of construction and remodeling companies: lead management, subcontractor coordination, and on-site project execution.

Key Challenges in Construction
and How Bitrix24 Solves Them

Business pain

Leads from multiple channels are getting lost

Bitrix24 Tools

CRM, Contact Center, CRM Forms

Practical Outcome

All leads from your website, ads, and referrals automatically flow into a single CRM — with no losses or duplicates. Better control of incoming leads and higher conversion rates.

Business pain

Lack of visibility into project and client status

Bitrix24 Tools

Tasks in CRM, Tasks in Projects, Kanban

Practical Outcome

Sales and project managers can track the real-time status of each client and project, with all tasks, responsible parties, and deadlines clearly defined within a single system.

Business pain

Challenges in managing subcontractors

Bitrix24 Tools

Extranet Users, Access Permissions

Practical Outcome

Subcontractors have access only to their tasks, with permissions automatically revoked upon project completion. Ensures full control and secure collaboration.

Business pain

Disorganized project documentation

Bitrix24 Tools

Drive, Online Documents, File version storage

Practical Outcome

Project documentation is centralized and linked to each client and job site, enabling better organization, fewer errors, and instant access to information.

Business pain

Lack of real-time information from the field

Bitrix24 Tools

Mobile App, Notifications

Practical Outcome

Receive real-time updates from the field, with tasks kept current, photos and documents always accessible, and immediate alerts for changes or delays.

Business pain

Limited control over project timelines and profitability

Bitrix24 Tools

CRM Analytics, Dashboards in BI Builder, Work Reports

Practical Outcome

Full control over project timelines, team workload, and profitability. Enhanced visibility leads to better financial outcomes.

Business pain

Manual, slow, and error-prone handoff from sales to project execution

Bitrix24 Tools

Workgroups and Projects, Automation in Scrum, Workflow Autorun

Practical Outcome

When a deal is closed, Bitrix24 automatically creates the project with all tasks, responsible parties, and deadlines. This enables a faster, more organized, and fully controlled project kickoff.

Business pain

Lack of coordination between field and office teams

Bitrix24 Tools

Group Chats, Shared Calendars, Flows

Practical Outcome

Field and office teams stay aligned in real time — with shared chats, calendars, and automated flows keeping everyone on the same page across every job site.
